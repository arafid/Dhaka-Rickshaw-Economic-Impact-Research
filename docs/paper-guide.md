# Paper guide

The original paper is 23 PDF pages including bibliography and appendices.

- **Pages 1-3:** research question, motivation, hypothesis, and methodology.
- **Pages 4-8:** primary interview data on income and expenses, economic interpretation, PPE use, and the six-month monthly-income/expense series.
- **Page 9:** regression analysis linking monthly income and monthly expenses.
- **Pages 10-11:** physical and mental-health discussion using interview evidence.
- **Pages 12-16:** poverty-trap and broader livelihood analysis.
- **Pages 16-17:** conclusion and limitations.
- **Pages 18-22:** bibliography and supporting sources.
- **Page 23:** interview questionnaire used for the primary research.

For portfolio use, the most important sections are the methodology, Tables 2-4, the regression analysis, the conclusion, and the limitations.
