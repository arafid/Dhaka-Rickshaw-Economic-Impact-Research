# Notice

This repository presents Afnan Rafid's original academic research as a portfolio/archive project.

The original PDF contains third-party news excerpts, figures, photographs, and source material that are credited in the paper's bibliography and appendices. Their inclusion in the historical paper does not grant a license to reuse those third-party materials outside the context of the original academic work.

The CSV files in `data/` are clean transcriptions of selected numeric tables from the author's original paper. They are provided for transparency and portfolio review, not as an official or complete dataset representing all rickshaw pullers in Dhaka.
